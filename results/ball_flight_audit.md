# Ball-flight plausibility audit (2026-10-17, MODEL-BASED, simulation only)

Question: at 132 mph club / ~92 m/s ball the evaluator reports ~310 m carry, and at 120 mph club ~112 m. Are distances too generous, and why?

Method: read the aero and impact code (`env/humanoid_golf_env.py` `_apply_aero`, post-contact `impact_model`; `env/ball_flight_analytic.py`; `eval/flight_tracker.py`; `sim/golf_simulator.py`), measured ball speed / launch / backspin at club-ball separation in the MuJoCo rollouts (`eval/measure_sim_spin` = `eval/measure_sep_spin.py`, 5-6 det episodes each, `results/ball_spin_measured.json`, tiny N, diagnostic only), re-flew the same inputs through `sim/golf_simulator.shot_from_launch` (validated: 167 mph / 10.9 deg / 2686 rpm -> 268 yd carry, -2.4% vs TrackMan 275; apex +4%; landing angle +1.8 deg), and compared with TrackMan PGA Tour averages (113 mph club, 167 mph ball, smash 1.48, 10.9 deg, 2686 rpm, 275 yd carry). Scripts: `/tmp`-free versions are `eval/measure_sep_spin.py` and `sim/launch_monitor_power.py`.

## Bottom line
1. **No unit error.** 93.3 m/s = 208.7 mph ball speed. 310 m = 339 yd. The analytic model fed the same launch (208.7 mph, 12.6 deg, 3088 rpm) gives 335 yd (306.6 m); the MuJoCo flight gives 310 m. The two flights agree to ~1% at these inputs.
2. **The 310 m is a consequence of inputs that are themselves hypothetical, not of a broken flight model:** (a) club speed 132 mph (power XML, force_scale 2, `t_down` 0.06 s; tour avg 113, long-drive pros ~140-150 head speed); (b) the power-tier env multiplies the contact ball velocity by `impact_model.gain` 1.28, giving **smash 1.55-1.59** against ~1.48-1.50 for a legal driver and 1.48 TrackMan avg. That makes ball speed 209 mph where a 1.48 smash would give 196 mph (316 yd carry in the model, ~5% shorter). Even with smash 1.48 a 132 mph club gives ~316 yd carry, so the distance is mostly the club speed. Ball speed 209 mph is at the very top of anything observed (long-drive).
3. **The non-power policies are the opposite: far too SHORT versus real golf.** In the v3 envs (imit15, imit16, imit9, imit3, scripted) the ball leaves at ~club speed (**smash ~0.99-1.01**) with **almost no backspin (17 rpm imit16, 362 rpm imit15, 920 rpm imit9; real ~2700)**. A real 120 mph club hits ~178 mph ball -> ~292 yd carry; the v3 env produces ~121 mph ball -> 114 m (125 yd). So the 112 m for 120 mph is not generous; it reflects smash 1.0 and no lift. Re-flying imit15's sim ball speed/launch through the analytic model at the sim's own 362 rpm gives 142 yd carry (MuJoCo 125 yd), at 2700 rpm 169 yd.
4. **Spin.** (a) Raw analytic `shot_from_club` impact spin is ~58% high (4259 vs 2686 rpm at tour avg; Data Agent R1, `SPIN_SCALE_TOUR_CAL = 0.62` is an opt-in correction, historical numbers unchanged). (b) The MuJoCo contact spin in v3 envs is tens to ~900 rpm (too low). (c) The power tier's spin is not produced by contact: it is set by `impact_model` (`spin_k` 130 x ball speed x sin(launch+2 deg)) = ~3080 rpm, about 15% above tour 2686 at a ball speed 25% above tour.
5. **Drag / lift.** MuJoCo env aero: Cd constant 0.26, Cl = min(1.7 S, 0.30), rho 1.19. Analytic model: Cd = max(0.17+0.60 S, 0.55-0.0085 v) (~0.22 at tour speeds), Cl = min(0.45, 0.42 S^0.4). At 93 m/s, 3050 rpm the two give 308 m (MuJoCo-style) vs 307 m (analytic); at 74.7 m/s / 2686 rpm 252 vs 246 m, i.e. similar at the speeds that matter for the power tier; Cd 0.22 in the MuJoCo aero would give 347 m. So the MuJoCo aero is a bit draggier than the analytic one, not generous.
6. **Launch and landing.** Launch 12.6 deg (power tier) / 11.5-12.7 deg (others): in the TrackMan 10-14 deg window. Modelled landing angle for the power tier ~53 deg, apex ~66 yd, hang ~9 s (TrackMan avg: 38-40 deg, 32 yd, 6.7 s): the high ball speed makes a high, steep flight, not a flat unrealistic one.
7. **Roll.** MuJoCo evaluation episodes end at first landing, so evaluator "total" equals carry (`mean_roll_m` = 0.0 in every gate JSON). The analytic model adds roll (imit18 inputs: 335 yd carry -> 360 yd total on a fairway). Evaluator totals therefore understate roll.

## Verdict and what was changed
- Distances are **too generous only for the power tier** (hypothetical club speed + smash 1.55-1.59 > legal limit); they are **too short for every v3 policy** (smash ~1, ~no spin). Neither is a flight-model bug: carry follows the inputs consistently (model vs MuJoCo within ~1% for the power tier). The unrealistic parts are the post-contact ball-velocity/spin inputs of the envs and the hypothetical-strength club speeds.
- No historical number was changed. Added instead: (1) leaderboard section "Sim carry vs launch-monitor-model carry" with MuJoCo carry, model carry at the sim's ball speed/launch (2700 rpm assumed) and model carry at smash 1.48 for every row, flagged MODEL-BASED; (2) `results/ball_spin_measured.json` + `eval/measure_sep_spin.py`; (3) `sim/launch_monitor_power.py` -> `results/launch_monitor_power_tier.{md,json}` per-shot launch-monitor view of imit18 and ppo_v4power48_s71.
- Not done / open: the power-tier `impact_model.gain` (1.28) could be reduced to ~1.0-1.05 so smash <= 1.48 (this would change the env, the Trainer's call); the v3 env's smash ~1.0 and near-zero spin would need a similar post-contact model to give real-like carry. Confidence: high for the unit/consistency findings, medium for spin numbers (6 episodes).
