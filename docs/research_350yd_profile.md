# 350-yard-class driving: researched power profile (compiled 2026-10-16/17)

Targets: `data/references/power_profile_350yd_targets.json` (generated by `scripts/gen_power_profile_350yd.py`, validated by `tests/test_power_profile_350yd.py`).
Every number below has a source id in the JSON `sources` block. "Verified" tags: **F** = page/PDF fetched and number read; **S** = number seen only in a search-result snippet (PDF/page not fetched); **D** = derived by us; **U** = unverified (not retrieved). No player videos were scraped. Bryson figures are vendor/media numbers, not radar data.

## 1. Rules and equipment
- Max club length 48 in (1.219 m), except putters. **F** [USGA Part 2 s.1c](https://www.usga.org/equipment-standards/equipment-rules-2019/equipment-rules/part-2-rule-2.html). Optional Local Rule G-10 limits to 46 in **S** ([USGA FAQ](https://www.usga.org/equipment-standards/equipment-faq-25852.html)). >48 in only with documented need + Local Committee approval **S** ([R&A](https://www.randa.org/en/equipment-info-for-players)).
- Ball Overall Distance Standard: 317 yd (+3) at 120 mph / 10 deg / 2520 rpm; from the 2028 cycle 125 mph / 11 deg / 2220 rpm. **F** [USGA ball FAQ](https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/advancing-the-game/distance-insights/golf-ball-revised-testing-guidelines--frequently-asked-questions.html). USGA expects 13-15 yd loss for longest hitters.
- Driver spring effect: CT 239 us + 18 us tolerance **S** ([USGA notice](https://www.usga.org/content/dam/usga/pdf/RSI/distance-insights-2021-docs/Notice%20%20-Spring%20Effect%20Interpretation%201-11-16%20Final.pdf)). 460 cc head: **U** (only a retail guide surfaced). Typical tour driver length/head mass/shaft specs: **U**.

## 2. Tour baselines and Bryson
- TrackMan PGA Tour driver avg: 113 mph club, 167 ball, smash 1.48, 10.9 deg, 2686 rpm, attack -1.3 deg, carry 251 m (275 yd). **F** [TrackMan PDF](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/7349802113051/PGA_Tour_average.pdf); method: competition+range, 200+ players **F** [blog](https://www.trackman.com/blog/introducing-updated-tour-averages). Blog lists smash 1.49, spin 2545 (different basis) **F** [smash](https://www.trackman.com/blog/smash-factor).
- DeChambeau 2024 avg drive 320.9 yd (ShotLink-derived, machine-written article) **F** [PGA Tour](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/betting-profile/2024/pga-championship/bryson-dechambeau-pga-tour-betting-stats-pga-championship-2024). 128 mph avg club speed is Sportsbox video-AI, tour group avg 117 **F** [Sportsbox](https://help.sportsbox.ai/dechambeaus-swing). 338 yd avg at 2024 US Open and ~172 mph avg ball speed: secondary media **F** [GOLF.com](https://golf.com/gear/how-far-bryson-dechambeau-hit-persimmon-open/). 2020 claims conflict (~140 mph/190 ball [theScore, S] vs "approaching 150" [Arccos, S]) - low confidence.

## 3. What ball speed / launch / spin gives ~350 yd total
Physics: smash is capped near (1+e)/(1+m_ball/m_head) = 1.83/1.23 = **1.49** (e=0.83, 200 g head, centre hit; **D**), matching tour 1.48-1.49. So distance must come from club speed: ball speed ~1.48 x club speed. Optimum launch falls and spin falls as speed rises (high launch, low spin: carry is lift-limited, roll adds ~25-30 yd).
- Foresight optimized table (vendor, optimistic): 190 mph ball (131 club) -> 10-14 deg, 1600-2600 rpm, carry 342-372, total 378-401 yd. **F** [Foresight](https://help.foresightsports.com/hc/en-us/articles/47208228295187-Ball-Launch-Data-Performance-Reference-Tables).
- Our repo flight model (`env/ball_flight_analytic.py`, calm, centre hit; calibration check 167 mph/10.9 deg/2686 rpm -> 268 yd carry vs 275 TrackMan, -2.5%) at smash 1.48, best of launch 11-14 deg / 2500-2000 rpm (**D**):

| club mph | ball mph | launch/spin | carry yd | total yd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 113 | 167 | 14/2000 | 279 | 307 |
| 120 | 178 | 14/2000 | 299 | 327 |
| **130** | 192 | 13/2100 | 324 | **351** |
| 140 | 207 | 14/2000 | 349 | 377 |
| 150 | 222 | 14/2000 | 371 | 399 |
So "350-class" = ~130 mph club / ~190 mph ball / ~13 deg / ~2100 rpm. Wind, altitude and turf can move this +/-20 yd (not modeled).

- **Wrist-only uncock speed: U.** Proxy only: arm->club gain 1274 deg/s scaled with head speed (~1520 deg/s at 130 mph, **D**, low).

## 5. Forces, torques, power
- Peak vertical GRF 1.6-2.0 BW, AP 0.4-0.6, ML 0.2-0.3 (Hume 2005, doi:10.2165/00007256-200535050-00005) **S**. Lead-foot GRF moment and trail-foot pivoting moment correlate with clubhead speed in 63 low-handicap men (Han/Kwon 2019, doi:10.1080/14763141.2019.1586983) **S**; **ground-reaction torque magnitudes: U** (not retrieved).
- Nesbit & Serrano 2005 **F** ([JSSM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3899668/); n=4 amateurs, one swing each): scratch golfer head 52.0 m/s, peak club power 3875 W (linear 2775, angular 1150), max grip torque 42.1 N*m, force 512 N, club KE 334 J, total joint work 1452 J (core: lumbar+thoracic+hips ~72%, arms ~25%, legs ~4%), efficiency ~25%. Higher speeds were noted to cost accuracy and be lossy.
- Scaling to 130/150 mph (**D**, extrapolation: power ~v^3, work ~v^2): club power 5.4 / 8.3 kW, total body work 1.8 / 2.4 kJ.

## 6. Humanoid requirements vs the MuJoCo model (`env/models/humanoid_golf_v3.xml`)
Peak torque = gear x forcerange; motors are torque-only, joint damping b is a passive loss, so net torque at rate w = tau - b*w, no-load speed = tau/b, max power = tau^2/(4b). Calculations are generated in the JSON (`humanoid_actuator_audit`).
| joint | tau N*m | b | no-load deg/s | max W | range deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shoulder pitch (R) | 250 | 2 | 7162 | 7812 | +-180 |
| shoulder roll (R) / L pitch | 150 | 2 | 4297 | 2812 | +-90 / +-180 |
| elbow (R) | 120 | 2 | 3438 | 1800 | 0-150 |
| **wrist (R)** | **60** | 2 | **1719** | **450** | +-120 |
| hip pitch | 250 | 2 | 7162 | 7812 | +-60 |
| knee / ankle | 200 / 80 | 2 | 5729 / 2292 | 4999 / 800 | |
| chest yaw | 120 | 1 | 6875 | 3600 | **+-45** |
Findings:
1. **Wrist is the bottleneck.** Needed uncock rate ~26.5 rad/s at 130 mph leaves 7 of 60 N*m (12%); at 150 mph the net torque is negative. For a 50% torque margin: damping <= 1.13 (130 mph) / 0.98 (150) with tau=60, or tau >= 106 / 122 N*m with damping 2. Wrist max power 450 W vs ~1.6 kW (Nesbit-scaled angular power at 130 mph): 3.6x short. Cheapest fix: cut wrist damping to <=0.5 (real wrist has far less passive drag) and raise to ~120 N*m.
2. **Chest yaw** is torque/speed-adequate (thorax 950 deg/s = 16.6 rad/s, 17 N*m drag of 120) but has +-45 deg range vs 94-112 deg real shoulder turn, and there is **no pelvis yaw DOF** (hips are sagittal pitch), so no X-factor (59-68 deg) or pelvis->thorax sequencing is possible. Add a pelvis yaw joint (~+-60 deg, >=500 deg/s, ~150 N*m) and widen chest to ~+-70 deg.
3. Shoulder/hip pitch actuators are large (250 N*m, 7.8 kW) relative to Nesbit's per-joint work, but sagittal-only.
4. Club in model: head 0.20 + shaft 0.10 kg, 1.12 m (~44 in.), inertia 0.291 kg*m^2 about wrist; rules allow up to 48 in., which gives ~9% more head speed at the same angular rate (D) at the cost of control.
5. Vertical GRF 1.6-2.0 BW should be checked once the model's total mass is known (mujoco not installed in this worktree; mass not computed).

## 7. Caveats, injury, overspeed
- L4-L5 compression 6.5-8+ BW right after impact, higher with modern X-factor swings; over-use dominates, back is the most common injury **F** ([Edwards 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9219256/)). Higher average club speed was associated with back injuries in a small PGA retrospective case-control (association only) **S** (doi:10.1080/00913847.2020.1809968). Overspeed protocols: vendor-affiliated randomized studies report speed gains but **no injury outcomes** **S** (Par4Success PDF); no independent injury trial found.
- Everything here is a prior from other people's bodies; single-view ~30 fps video (our Mason refs) cannot verify club speed or these segment speeds.
- Bands are plausibility ranges, not measured data. Vendor tables (Foresight, Sportsbox) are not peer reviewed.
