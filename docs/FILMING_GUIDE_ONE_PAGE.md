# Filming guide (one page): Mason's next swing session

Full detail: `docs/FILMING_PROTOCOL.md`. Camera numbers are practical defaults, not validated (unverified).

**Why:** the feed clips are ~30 fps and edited, so they can't measure X-factor, sequencing, club speed or attack angle. A 240 fps session with launch-monitor numbers fixes that.

**Bring:** 2 phones, 2 tripods, a launch monitor, a 1 m stick or two tape marks 1.00 m apart, painter's tape, and a loud clap. Bright daylight, fitted top, long pants.

**Phone settings (both phones):** Slo-mo, **1080p at 240 fps**, 1x lens (move the tripod, don't zoom), no stabilisation, no cinematic mode, focus and exposure locked on you. Landscape. Send the **original** files by AirDrop, USB or iCloud. No screen recordings and no Instagram.

**Two angles (you're left-handed, so your lead side is your right):**
- **Down-the-line:** behind the ball on the target line, ~1 m high, ~3-4 m back. Full body, club and follow-through in frame.
- **Face-on:** square to the target line, ~1 m high, ~3-4 m away. Head to feet, with margin at the top of the swing.
Tape the tripod feet and ball spot. Never move the cameras mid-session.

**Sync:** film the 1 m stick at the ball for 3 s per camera. Start both cameras, then clap once in view of both before every ~10-swing block. Keep rolling until 2 s after impact.

**Launch-monitor numbers, every swing:** club speed, ball speed, smash, launch, spin, attack angle, club path, face angle, carry, total. Leave blanks blank; never guess. Keep mishits (rate strike 1-5).

**Write down once:** driver model, stamped and measured loft, length (in), shaft flex and weight (g), grip, **tee height** (in) and ball position, mat or turf, wind, monitor model, `handedness=left`. Use one driver per session.

**Plan (~40 swings):** warm up 10-15 min off camera. Then 10 swings at ~90%, 10 at max intent (your "120+"), 10 normal, and 10 optional drill swings. Rest 60-90 s between swings. Rate fatigue every 5 swings and stop at 4. Keep the bad swings too.

**Naming:** `mason_<YYYYMMDD>_driver_<dtl|fo>_<NNN>.mov`, for example `mason_20261024_driver_dtl_007.mov` and `mason_20261024_driver_fo_007.mov`. The same NNN in both views means the same swing. Log each swing in `data/templates/session_log.csv`.

**Rules:** only you in the footage, no bystanders, no music or branded overlays. Anyone else who swings needs a signed release. Raw videos stay out of git.

**Unknown:** whether 1080p240 is enough for our pose model at 3-4 m. We'll find out from the first clips. Club speed claims come from the monitor, not the video.
